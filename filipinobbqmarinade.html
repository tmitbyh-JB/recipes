Filipino BBQ Marinade

Yield: 2 Cups, 32 Servings (1 Tbsp) Prep: 15 Minutes Cook: None

[ Insert Photo Here ]

Ingredients

  • Soy Sauce — ½ Cup
  • Banana Ketchup — ½ Cup
  • Lemon Juice — 1 Lemon
  • Lemon Lime Flavored Soda — ½ Cup
  • Garlic — 1 Bulb, Crushed or Diced
  • Brown Sugar — ¼ Cup
  • Black Pepper — 1 Tablespoon

Instructions

  1. Whisk all the ingredients in a medium bowl until the brown sugar dissolves, 1–2 minutes.
  2. Transfer ½ cup of the marinade to a small covered container and refrigerate for glazing.
  3. Combine the remaining marinade with up to 3 lb meat in a gallon zip-top bag, press out the air, and seal. Refrigerate 8–24 hours, turning the bag once
  4. Remove the meat from the bag, letting the excess drip off, and discard the used marinade. Brush the meat with the reserved marinade during the last 2–3 minutes of cooking.
